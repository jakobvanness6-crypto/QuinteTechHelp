# Job intake and handover checklist

Run this on every job. It takes five minutes and protects you and the customer.

## Before you touch it
- [ ] Customer name and best contact, logged in the job tracker
- [ ] Device make, model and serial number written down
- [ ] Problem in the customer's own words
- [ ] Photos of the device from all sides, showing existing scratches, cracks and missing keys
- [ ] Accessories received (charger, cables, case, drives) listed
- [ ] Does it power on right now? Note it either way
- [ ] Password or PIN, if the job needs one. Don't write it in the tracker; delete it when the job closes
- [ ] Asked: "Is there anything on here you can't afford to lose?" and "When was it last backed up?"
- [ ] Customer has agreed to the quote in writing (a text or message counts)
- [ ] If the job could affect their files: backup plan agreed in writing

## While you work
- [ ] Back up first if a reinstall, drive swap or repair could put data at risk
- [ ] Note what you found and what you changed, as you go
- [ ] If the price or plan changes: stop, message the customer, wait for a yes
- [ ] Keep any replaced parts to hand back

## Before it goes back
- [ ] Problem confirmed fixed; device restarted and re-tested at least once
- [ ] Windows updated, nothing left half-installed
- [ ] Your tools, installers and temporary accounts removed
- [ ] Any backup copy you made is returned, then deleted from your equipment once they confirm they have their files
- [ ] Screen and case wiped down
- [ ] Plain-English summary ready: what was wrong, what you did, how to avoid it
- [ ] All accessories from the intake list going back

## Closing out
- [ ] Payment received and logged
- [ ] Job tracker status set to done
- [ ] Follow-up message set for 3 to 5 days out
- [ ] If they're happy: ask for the Google review and the referral
