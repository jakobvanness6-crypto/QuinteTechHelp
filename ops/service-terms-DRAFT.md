# Service terms (DRAFT, not published)

This is a starting point, not legal advice. It is deliberately NOT on the website yet.
Decide the items marked DECIDE, then have someone qualified glance at it before you rely on it.
Once it's settled it can become a /terms/ page and a line on your quotes.

## Decisions only you can make
- DECIDE: Diagnostic fee. Free, a flat amount, or free only if they go ahead with the repair?
- DECIDE: "No fix, no fee"? Strong selling point, but it means some unpaid hours.
- DECIDE: Warranty on your labour. A common choice for small shops is 30 days on the specific problem fixed.
- DECIDE: Payment methods (e-transfer, cash) and when payment is due (on completion is typical).
- DECIDE: How long you'll hold an uncollected device, and what happens after.
- DECIDE: House-call travel. Included within a set area, or a fee beyond it?

## Draft wording

**Quotes.** You get a quote before any work starts. If I find something that changes the price, I stop and ask before continuing.

**Parts.** Parts are paid for before I order them. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, not mine.

**Your data.** You are responsible for backing up your data before a repair. I take care with your files and will warn you before any step that could affect them, and I can do a backup for you as part of the job if you ask. I can't be held responsible for data loss on a device that was already failing.

**Your privacy.** I only access what the job requires. See quintetechhelp.ca/privacy.

**Passwords.** If the job needs your password or PIN, I use it only for the job and don't keep it afterwards. Changing it after the repair is always a good idea.

**Existing condition.** I note and photograph the condition of your device when I receive it. Older or damaged devices can fail during diagnosis or repair through no fault of the work; I'll tell you up front if I think yours is at risk.

**Warranty on labour.** [DECIDE] If the same problem comes back within [30] days, I'll look at it again at no charge. This doesn't cover new problems, physical or liquid damage, or software installed afterwards.

**Payment.** [DECIDE] Payment is due when the work is complete, by [e-transfer or cash].

**Uncollected devices.** [DECIDE] Devices not picked up within [30/60/90] days of the "ready" message, after reasonable attempts to reach you, may be [stored at a fee / recycled].

**What I don't do.** I don't bypass passwords or locks on devices you can't show are yours, and I don't install pirated software.
