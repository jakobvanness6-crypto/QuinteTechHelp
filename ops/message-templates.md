# Customer message templates

Copy, fill in the [brackets], send. Written to work as a text, Messenger message or email.
Keep the habit: one message at every status change, so nobody has to chase you.

## 1. First reply to a new request
Hi [name], thanks for reaching out to Quinte Tech Help. Sorry to hear about the [device]. A couple of quick questions so I can give you an accurate quote:
- Roughly how old is it, and what make/model if you know?
- When did the problem start, and did anything happen right before (update, drop, spill, new program)?
- Is there anything on it you can't afford to lose?
Once I have that I'll send you a quote and we can sort out drop-off or a house call.

## 2. Quote
Hi [name], here's the quote for your [device]:
- What I'll do: [plain-English description]
- Labour: $[amount]
- Parts: [none / part name, $amount, paid before I order]
- Total: $[amount]
- Timing: [about X days once I have it / done on the visit]
Nothing starts until you say go. If I find anything that changes the price once I'm in there, I stop and check with you first.

## 3. Booking confirmed
Great, you're booked. [Drop-off: bring the device and its charger to [place] on [day] at [time]. / House call: I'll be at [address] on [day] around [time].] If it has a password or PIN, have it handy. If anything changes on your end, just message me here.

## 4. Device received
Got your [device], thanks [name]. I'll start with a diagnosis and message you [today/tomorrow] with what I find before doing anything else.

## 5. Diagnosis update
Hi [name], I've had a look at your [device]. In plain English: [what's wrong]. The fix is [what I'll do]. That [matches the quote / changes the quote to $amount because reason]. OK to go ahead?

## 6. Waiting on a part
Quick update: the [part] is ordered and due [date]. I'll message you the day it lands and should have you finished [timing] after that.

## 7. Ready
Good news, your [device] is ready. [One line on what was done.] Total is $[amount]. When works for [pickup / me to bring it back]?

## 8. It can't be fixed (or isn't worth fixing)
Hi [name], I want to be straight with you. [What's wrong], and fixing it would cost about $[amount], which is more than the [device] is worth. My honest recommendation is [replace it / option B]. I can [recover your files / help you pick a replacement / set up the new one] if that would help.

## 9. Follow-up, 3 to 5 days after the job
Hi [name], just checking in on the [device]. Everything still running the way it should?

## 10. Review ask (send only after a happy reply to #9)
Glad to hear it! If you have a minute, a Google review makes a huge difference for a small local business like mine: [Google review link]. And if you know anyone with a tech headache, send them to quintetechhelp.ca.

## 11. Payment reminder
Hi [name], a friendly reminder about the $[amount] for the [device] work on [date]. [Payment method details]. Thanks!

## 12. Not something I do
Thanks for asking, [name]. That one's outside what I handle, and I'd rather tell you now than waste your time. [Suggestion if you have one.] If anything else comes up with your PC or laptop, I'm happy to help.
