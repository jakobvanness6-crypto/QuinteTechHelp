New computer? Let's set it up right the first time.

A new PC or laptop should be exciting, not a weekend of frustration. I get it updated and secured, bring your files over from the old one, and make sure the printer and Wi-Fi are working before I leave.

Good for

  • A new PC or laptop straight out of the box
  • Moving everything from an old computer to a new one
  • A printer or Wi-Fi connection that won't cooperate
  • Setting up backups so your photos and documents are safe
  • Parents or grandparents who'd like patient, one-on-one help

What I do

  • Run the updates and remove the trial software new computers ship with
  • Set up your accounts and email
  • Transfer your documents, photos and bookmarks
  • Connect the printer, Wi-Fi and anything else you use
  • Show you around, at your pace, until you're comfortable

What it costs

Most jobs fall between $50 and $100 for the work. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately. You always get a clear quote before anything starts, and nothing happens until you approve it.

"Can you just show me how?"

Yes. Some visits are less about fixing and more about learning. If you want someone to sit down and walk you through something without rushing or jargon, that's part of the job.

How long it takes

Most setups take a single visit or a quick drop-off. Moving a lot of files from an old computer can take longer, and I'll give you an idea of timing when we talk.

Questions people ask

What should I have ready?

Your Wi-Fi password, the email and password for your main accounts (like your Microsoft or Google account), and the old computer if we're moving files across. If you've lost a password, tell me and we'll work through it.

Do you help with phones and tablets too?

Windows computers are my main thing, but I help with phones, tablets and other devices too. If you're not sure, ask and I'll tell you straight.

What happens to my old computer?

Your files stay on it until you've checked that everything made it across. If you're getting rid of it, I can wipe it properly so your personal stuff doesn't leave with it.

Where I work

I'm based in Belleville and help people across Quinte West, Trenton, Prince Edward County and the rest of the Bay of Quinte. House calls are often easiest for setup, since your Wi-Fi and printer are at home.

Other ways I can help

  • Laptop upgrades: the old laptop might be worth reviving instead of replacing
  • Virus and malware removal: for pop-ups, scams and a PC that's acting strange
