Pop-ups, fake warnings, a PC that's not acting right?

Viruses and unwanted programs slow your computer down, hijack your browser and try to scare you into paying for things you don't need. I clean them out properly and explain how they got in, so it's less likely to happen again.

Signs something's on there

  • Pop-ups or "your PC is infected" warnings that won't go away
  • Your homepage or search engine changed on its own
  • Programs you never installed
  • The PC suddenly got much slower, or the fan runs constantly
  • Friends are getting strange emails or messages from you

What I do

  • Find and remove the infection and the junk that came with it
  • Check startup programs and browser extensions for anything that shouldn't be there
  • Make sure Windows and its built-in protection are up to date and switched on
  • Show you what to watch for next time

What it costs

Most jobs fall between $50 and $100 for the work. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately. You always get a clear quote before anything starts, and nothing happens until you approve it.

A warning about scam pop-ups

If a pop-up or a caller says your computer is infected and asks for remote access or payment, don't call the number and don't let them in. Microsoft doesn't contact people that way. Close the browser, and if someone already got access, turn the computer off and get in touch. It's also worth calling your bank.
