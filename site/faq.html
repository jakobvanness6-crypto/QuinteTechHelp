Questions, answered straight

The stuff people usually ask before handing over their computer. If yours isn't here, just email me.

Timing

How long will my repair take?

Most software jobs, like virus removal, tune-ups and clean reinstalls, are done within 1 to 3 days. Upgrades are often same-day or next-day once the part is in hand. If a part has to be ordered, I'll give you the timeline with your quote, and I'll message you if anything changes.

How quickly will I hear back?

I usually reply within a few hours of a request coming in.

Your files

Will my files be safe?

Nothing gets wiped without you agreeing to it first. Before any job that could touch your files, like a reinstall or a drive swap, I back them up or we agree on a plan. I only open what the job needs, and any backup copy I make is deleted once you've confirmed you have your files back. If you have a recent backup of your own, even better.

Should I back up before I bring it in?

If you can, yes. Copy your photos and documents to a USB drive or cloud storage. If the computer won't start or you're not sure how, tell me when you book and I can do the backup as part of the job.

Drop-off and house calls

Where do I drop off my device?

Drop-off is in Belleville. I'll send you the address and a time that works when we book. House calls are also available around Belleville, Quinte West, Trenton and Prince Edward County.

What should I bring?

The device, its charger, and your password or PIN if the job needs you to be logged in (change it afterwards if you like, that's always a good habit). Bring any discs, drives or parts that relate to the problem too. Remove anything you don't need me to have, like SD cards or extra USB sticks.

Money and guarantees

How much does a repair cost?

Diagnosis is free and most jobs fall between $30 and $80 (see the price list). You always get a quote before any work starts, and nothing happens until you approve it. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately.

How do I pay?

Payment is due when the work is done, by Interac e-Transfer or cash. If your repair needs a part, I'll ask you to cover the part before I order it. You'll always see the price in your quote first.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. If the same problem comes back within 30 days, I'll look at it again at no charge. That doesn't cover new problems, drops or spills, or software installed afterwards. Parts carry their manufacturer's warranty.
