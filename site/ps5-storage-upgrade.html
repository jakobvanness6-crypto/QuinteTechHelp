PS5 full again? Stop deleting games.

The PS5 has a slot inside for a second solid-state drive, and filling it is the best way to add room for games. I install the drive, set it up and move your games over so you can get back to playing.

Good to know

  • The PS5 needs a specific kind of drive: an M.2 NVMe SSD that meets Sony's speed and size requirements, with a heatsink
  • Not every SSD on the shelf will work, so ask before you buy and I'll tell you which ones fit
  • Your saves and games stay put; the new drive adds space alongside the built-in storage

What I do

  • Help you choose a compatible drive for your budget
  • Install it in the console's expansion slot
  • Format it and move games across
  • Test it before it goes back to you

What it costs

Most jobs fall between $50 and $100 for the work. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately. You always get a clear quote before anything starts, and nothing happens until you approve it.

Already bought a drive?

No problem. Send me the make and model in the request form and I'll confirm it's compatible before you hand over the console.
