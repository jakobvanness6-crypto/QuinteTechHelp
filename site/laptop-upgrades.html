Slow laptop? It probably doesn't need replacing.

Most older laptops are slow for two fixable reasons: an old spinning hard drive and not enough memory. Swapping in a solid-state drive, adding RAM or starting fresh with a clean copy of Windows can make a tired laptop feel new, for a fraction of what a replacement costs.

Signs an upgrade will help

  • It takes minutes to start up or open a program
  • You hear the drive grinding or clicking away while you wait
  • It bogs down with a few browser tabs open
  • It was fine when you bought it and has slowly gotten worse

What I do

  • Check the laptop first, so you know an upgrade is worth it before you spend anything
  • Tell you exactly which drive or memory fits your model
  • Move Windows and your files to the new drive, or do a clean reinstall if that's the better fix
  • Test everything and walk you through what changed

What it costs

Diagnosis is free and most jobs fall between $30 and $80 for the work. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately. See the full price list. You always get a clear quote before anything starts, and nothing happens until you approve it.

And if it isn't worth fixing?

Then I'll say so. Some laptops are too old or too damaged to be worth the money, and I'd rather tell you up front. I can help you pick a sensible replacement and move your files over.

How long it takes

Upgrades are often done same-day or next-day once the part is in hand. If a drive or memory has to be ordered, you'll get the timeline with your quote, and I'll message you if anything changes.

Questions people ask

Is an SSD really that much faster?

For a laptop that still has an old spinning hard drive, it's usually the single biggest improvement you can make. Starting up and opening programs goes from minutes to seconds.

Can every laptop be upgraded?

Most laptops can take a new drive. Memory is trickier, because some newer, thinner laptops have it soldered to the board. I'll check your exact model and tell you what's possible before you buy anything.

Will I lose my files?

No. Your files are copied onto the new drive, or backed up first if we're doing a clean reinstall. Nothing gets wiped without you agreeing to it first.
