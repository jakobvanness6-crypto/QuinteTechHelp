Free PC check-up app: Help Me Help You

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store

One click checks your PC and saves a report you can send to whoever helps you. It covers your Windows version, drive space and health, startup programs, failed updates, battery wear and recent crashes, with each item marked OK, Worth a look or Needs attention.

It works offline, changes nothing on your PC, never reads your files, and removes your username, PC name and serial numbers from the report. Until it's in the Store, just describe the problem in the form below and I'll walk you through it.
