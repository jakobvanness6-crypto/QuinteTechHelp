Free PC check-up app: Help Me Help You

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store

One click checks your PC and saves a report you can send to whoever helps you. It covers your Windows version, drive space and health, startup programs, failed updates, battery wear and recent crashes, with each item marked OK, Worth a look or Needs attention.

It works offline, changes nothing on your PC, never reads your files, and removes your username, PC name and serial numbers from the report. Until it's in the Store, just describe the problem in the form below and I'll walk you through it.

Common questions

How much does a repair cost?

Most jobs fall between $50 and $100. You always get a quote before any work starts, and nothing happens until you approve it. Parts, if you need any, are quoted separately.

Do you come to me, or do I drop my device off?

Either. Drop-off and house calls are both available around Belleville, Quinte West, Trenton and Prince Edward County. Pick whichever suits you in the request form, or choose "Not sure yet" and we'll figure it out.

Will I lose my files?

Nothing gets wiped without you agreeing to it first. If a job could affect your files, we talk about backing them up before I start.

What can you fix?

Windows desktops and laptops are the bread and butter: slow PCs, viruses, crashes, boot and update problems, upgrades and clean reinstalls. I also do PS5 storage upgrades, new device setup, Wi-Fi and printer trouble. If you're not sure it's something I handle, ask. I'll tell you straight if it's not.

How quickly will I hear back?

I usually reply within a few hours of a request coming in.
