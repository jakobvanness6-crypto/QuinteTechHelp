Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Quinte Tech Help is a small local repair service in Belleville, Ontario. This page explains, in plain English, what happens to your information.

What the request form collects

When you send a request, the form collects what you type into it: your name, your phone number or email, the kind of device, your description of the problem, whether you'd like drop-off or a house call, and how you heard about me if you choose to say.

What it's used for

Only to reply to you, quote the job, do the work and follow up on it. Your details are never sold, traded or added to a marketing list.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms delivers the form submission to my inbox.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and forwards mail sent to hello@quintetechhelp.ca. Like any web host, it processes basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
  • Google (Gmail) is the inbox where requests and replies are stored.

These services may process data outside Canada.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't run advertising trackers and doesn't set cookies of its own.

The data on your device

Fixing a computer sometimes means seeing what's on it. I only open what the job requires, I don't copy your personal files unless you've asked me to back up or transfer them, and any copy made for a backup or transfer is deleted once you've confirmed you have your files back.

Your choices

You can ask what information I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email hello@quintetechhelp.ca and I'll take care of it.

Ready to get it fixed?

Hit the button and your email opens with a short template. Fill in the blanks and I'll reply with a quote, usually within a few hours.
