## Getting reviews
1. In your profile, choose "Ask for reviews" to get your short review link. Save it in your phone notes.
2. Ask in person at handover, then send the link in the follow-up message (template 10 in ops/message-templates.md).
3. Only ask real customers, never offer anything in exchange (it's against Google's rules), and reply to every review.

**Reply to a good review:** Thanks so much, [name]! Glad the [device] is back to behaving. Appreciate you taking the time.

**Reply to a critical review:** Thanks for the honest feedback, [name]. I'm sorry this wasn't the experience you expected. I'd like to make it right; please email me at hello@quintetechhelp.ca.
