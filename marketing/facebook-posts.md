Paste as-is. Facebook builds the preview card from the link automatically
(that's what site/og-image.png is for, once the new site is deployed).

Tips:
- Many local groups only allow business posts on certain days. Check the pinned rules first.
- Don't paste the identical post into ten groups in one sitting; Facebook can flag it as spam. Space them out and rotate the versions below.
- Reply to every comment, even just "sent you a message". Activity keeps the post visible.
